Property management outgrew the spreadsheet a long time ago. Rent tracked in one notebook, maintenance requests in a phone thread, lease renewal dates that only exist in someone's memory — it works, until the day it very visibly doesn't. That's the gap Zoneer PropTech is built to close: rent, maintenance, leases, and tenants, in one system instead of scattered across notebooks and Telegram threads.

It looks different depending on who's using it. Landlords get one place to see every property — what's paid, what's due, what needs attention today. Tenants get a clear record instead of chasing a landlord for a receipt or a repair update; it's tracked, and both sides can see the same thing.

Here's the part worth calling out: **you won't find a signup form for it on this website.** zoneer.pro is where you learn what PropTech is and who it's for. The actual system lives at a separate address, [landlord.zoneer.pro](https://landlord.zoneer.pro) — a real, independent platform, not a page bolted onto the marketing site.

That separation is deliberate. A property management system and a company website have different jobs, different release cadences, and different audiences. Keeping them apart means the platform can move at its own pace without every product change turning into a website deploy.

If you're a landlord curious what running your properties on one system looks like, the best next step is to visit the platform directly rather than read about it secondhand.
